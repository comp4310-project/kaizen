# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a student team. We work together, we disagree about technical choices, and we
keep it respectful.

Expected of everyone:

- Treat teammates with respect, in the repository and everywhere else.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This query will not scale" is feedback. "You never
  think things through" is not.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding someone was careless.
- Carry your share, and say early when you cannot. A blocked teammate who speaks up on
  Monday is a scheduling problem. One who goes quiet until Friday is a crisis.
- Give credit plainly.

Not acceptable: harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private
information, or sustained disruption of the team's work.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, pull requests, issues, team chat, meetings, and any
context where you are representing the team.

## Reporting

Raise it with the team directly if you can. If that is not workable, contact the course
instructor or a teaching assistant.

Persistent problems that the team cannot resolve internally go to the course instructor.
