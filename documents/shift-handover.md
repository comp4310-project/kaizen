# Purpose

Information needed for safe care is passed on at every change of shift and every transfer.

# Bedside handover

Nursing handover takes place at the bedside where possible, so the patient can take part and correct mistakes.

Handover uses the unit's structured SBAR form: situation, background, assessment and recommendation.

# What handover must include

Handover includes the patient's current condition, any change in the last shift, falls and pressure injury risk, pending tests and results, medications due or held, and the patient's and family's concerns.

When a patient moves between units, the sending nurse phones the receiving nurse and gives handover before the patient leaves.

# Physician handover

Physicians hand over patients they are concerned about in person or by phone, and list pending results with the name of the person who will follow them up.

# Measures

The unit audits five handovers each month against the handover form and tracks incidents where missing handover information was a cause.
