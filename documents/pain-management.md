# Purpose

Patients' pain is taken seriously, assessed regularly and treated promptly.

# Assessment

Nurses ask about pain at least every 4 hours while the patient is awake and record a pain score using a tool suited to the patient.

When a patient or family member says that pain is getting worse, the nurse assesses the patient straight away and records what was reported and what was done.

# Treatment

Pain is reassessed within 60 minutes after giving pain medication, and the result is recorded.

If pain is not controlled after two doses or two hours, the nurse contacts the prescriber or the acute pain service.

# Listening to patients

Staff believe and act on what patients say about their own pain, and explain the plan when a request for medication cannot be met.

# Measures

The unit audits charts for reassessment within 60 minutes and reviews complaints that mention pain being ignored at the monthly quality meeting.
