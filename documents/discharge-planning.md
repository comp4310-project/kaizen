# Purpose

Discharge is planned from admission so patients do not wait on the day they are ready to leave.

# On admission

An expected date of discharge is set within 24 hours of admission and written on the patient's whiteboard.

The charge nurse confirms who will collect the patient and whether home care or equipment is needed, and books transport two days before the expected date where it is needed.

# Daily review

At the morning bed huddle the team reviews every patient due to leave in the next 48 hours and names the task, owner and time for anything that could delay discharge.

Patients and families are told the expected date of discharge and any change to it on the same day the change is made.

# Discharge before 11:00

Units aim to discharge patients before 11:00 so beds are available for patients waiting in the emergency department. Tests, consults and prescriptions needed for discharge are completed the day before wherever possible.

# Measures

The unit tracks the share of discharges completed before 11:00 and the number of hours between a patient being medically ready and leaving the unit.
