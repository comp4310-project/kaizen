# Purpose

Patients receive the right medication, at the right dose and time, and errors are reported and learned from.

# Before giving a medication

Nurses check two patient identifiers, the medication, dose, route and time against the medication record before every dose.

High-alert medications such as insulin, heparin and opioid infusions are checked independently by a second nurse before they are given.

# Missed and late doses

Time-critical medications are given within 30 minutes of the scheduled time. A dose that is missed or more than 2 hours late is reported to the prescriber and recorded as a medication incident.

# Reporting

Staff report every medication error and near miss in the incident system. Reports are reviewed weekly by the unit manager and pharmacist, and themes are shared at the safety huddle.

# Measures

The unit reports medication incidents per 1,000 doses and the share of time-critical doses given on time.
