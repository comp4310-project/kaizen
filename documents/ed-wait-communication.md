# Purpose

Patients and families in the emergency department waiting room know how long they may wait and are reassessed while they wait.

# Updates while waiting

The triage nurse tells each patient at triage what happens next and the current expected wait.

A nurse updates patients in the waiting room at least every 60 minutes on their place in the queue and the reason for any delay.

The waiting room screen shows the current expected wait for patients who are not seriously ill, updated every 30 minutes.

# Reassessment

Patients are reassessed while waiting at intervals set by their triage level, and at once if they or their family report that they feel worse.

Any patient whose condition has changed is retriaged and the change is recorded in the chart.

# Measures

The department audits a sample of waiting-room charts each week for reassessment on time, and runs a short survey asking waiting patients whether they were kept informed.
