# Purpose

Patients receive meals they can eat, at the right time, with help where they need it.

# Protected mealtimes

Non-urgent tests, rounds and procedures are not scheduled during main meal times, so patients are not interrupted while eating.

# Help with meals

Patients who need help to eat are identified on admission and have a red tray. A staff member or volunteer helps them at every meal.

Meals are served hot, and a patient who missed a meal because of a test or procedure is offered a replacement.

# Diet and preferences

Dietary needs, allergies and cultural or religious preferences are recorded on admission and sent to food services the same day.

# Measures

The unit audits meal assistance for red-tray patients each week and reviews food complaints with food services monthly.
