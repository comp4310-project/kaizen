# Purpose

Rooms and bathrooms are clean, and problems with the environment are fixed quickly.

# Cleaning schedule

Patient bathrooms are cleaned at least twice a day, including weekends and holidays, and more often for patients with infections.

Environmental services staff sign a checklist on the bathroom door after each clean, and the charge nurse checks a sample of rooms each day.

# Reporting problems

Patients, families and staff can report a dirty room, broken equipment or a spill to the unit desk at any time. Spills and soiled bathrooms are cleaned within 1 hour of being reported, and other requests within 4 hours.

# Measures

The unit tracks the share of cleaning checklists completed and the time from report to clean for urgent requests.
