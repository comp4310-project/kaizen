# Purpose

Patients are treated with respect and their privacy is protected.

# Private conversations

Staff discuss diagnoses, test results and personal information in a private room or at the bedside with the curtain closed and voices lowered, never in corridors or shared bays where others can hear.

Patients are asked who may be told about their care, and their answer is recorded in the chart.

# Personal care

Curtains are closed and the patient is covered during personal care and examinations.

Staff introduce themselves by name and role, and ask how the patient would like to be addressed.

# Concerns about staff behaviour

Patients and families who feel they were treated rudely can raise it with the charge nurse or the patient relations office. The manager follows up with the staff member and tells the patient what was done.

# Measures

The unit reviews survey comments and complaints about privacy and respect each month.
