# Purpose

Patients learn the results of their tests and no result is lost when a patient leaves hospital.

# During the stay

The physician or nurse practitioner tells the patient the results of scans and important tests within one working day of the result being available, or explains why it will take longer.

# Results pending at discharge

Any test result still pending at discharge is listed on the discharge summary with the name of the clinician who will follow it up.

The responsible clinician reviews each pending result when it arrives and contacts the patient and the family doctor about any result that changes care.

# Measures

The team audits discharge summaries for a complete list of pending results and a named clinician, and counts complaints that mention results not being passed on.
