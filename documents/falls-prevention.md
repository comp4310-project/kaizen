# Purpose

Fewer patients fall, and those at risk are known to every member of the team.

# Screening

Every patient is screened for falls risk within 24 hours of admission, after a fall, and after any change in condition or transfer between units.

Patients at high risk have a falls sign at the bedside and a falls risk flag on the electronic whiteboard.

# Prevention

The bed is kept at its lowest height with brakes on, and non-slip footwear and walking aids are within reach.

Patients at high risk are helped to the toilet on a regular schedule rather than waiting for them to call.

# Handover

Falls risk and any fall in the last 24 hours are included in every shift handover and every transfer between units.

# After a fall

After any fall the team holds a short huddle within the same shift to find out what happened and what will change.

# Measures

The unit reports falls per 1,000 patient days and the share of patients screened within 24 hours of admission.
