# Purpose

Patients can sleep at night, since poor sleep slows recovery and is one of the most common complaints about ward stays.

# Quiet hours

Quiet hours on inpatient units run from 22:00 to 06:00. Overhead lights in corridors are dimmed and overhead paging is used only for emergencies.

Staff keep conversations at the nursing station and in corridors quiet during quiet hours, and close patient room doors where it is safe to do so.

# Alarms and routine care

Monitor and pump alarms are set to the lowest safe volume at night, and nurses respond to alarms promptly so they do not ring for long.

Routine vital signs, blood tests and medication rounds are scheduled outside quiet hours unless the patient's condition needs them.

# Patient comfort

Earplugs and eye masks are offered to every patient at the start of quiet hours.

# Measures

The charge nurse records a noise check once each night. The unit also tracks the share of patients who report in survey comments that noise kept them awake.
