# Purpose

Patients who have been admitted but are still in the emergency department receive the same care as on a ward and move to a bed as soon as possible.

# Bed flow

The patient flow coordinator holds bed huddles at 08:00 and 14:00 with every inpatient unit to match expected discharges with patients waiting for beds.

An admitted patient who has waited more than 8 hours for a bed is escalated to the on-call manager, who reviews options such as transfer to another unit.

# Care while waiting

Admitted patients waiting in the department receive regular meals, their usual medications on time, and a bed or stretcher rather than a chair.

The emergency nurse tells the patient and family which unit they are waiting for and gives an update at least every 2 hours.

# Measures

The hospital reports the number of hours admitted patients spend in the emergency department and the share who wait more than 8 hours.
