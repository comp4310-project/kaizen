# Purpose

Patients get help when they need it and do not have to wait long after using the call bell.

# Purposeful rounding

Nurses or care aides visit every patient at least every hour during the day and every two hours overnight, checking pain, position, toileting needs and that personal items and the call bell are within reach.

Each round is recorded on the rounding sheet at the bedside.

# Answering call bells

Any member of the care team who hears a call bell answers it, even when the patient is not on their assignment.

Call bells are answered within 5 minutes. If the person answering cannot meet the need, they tell the patient who will come and when.

# Measures

The unit downloads call bell response times from the nurse call system each week and reviews the share answered within 5 minutes at the daily safety huddle.
